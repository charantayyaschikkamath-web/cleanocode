## 2. Product strategy independent of unknown rubric

Product thesis: **CampusTwin AI connects evidence-backed resource anomalies to human-approved interventions, independently verifies the outcome, and records only defensible impact.**

Operational loop: **Observe → Detect → Explain → Decide → Act → Independently verify → Quantify impact.**

Our candidate differentiation is the integrated workflow and the trust boundary between a command acknowledgement and independently verified outcome—not an unsupported claim that any one component is globally novel.

## 5. Evidence standard for the pitch

Every factual claim or number must be one of:

- **Verified external fact:** citation to official/publisher source.
- **Actual implementation result:** command output, test report, or screenshot from the release candidate.
- **Synthetic test result:** generated by the deterministic scenario evaluator and labelled as synthetic.
- **Projection:** deterministic formula with inputs, units, assumptions, and period shown.
- **Unvalidated hypothesis:** described as a hypothesis, not as a finding.

Do not claim a probability of winning, a field-validated accuracy score, campus savings, deployed integrations, customer adoption, or partnerships without evidence. Simulation success does not establish real-world detector accuracy or campus savings.
